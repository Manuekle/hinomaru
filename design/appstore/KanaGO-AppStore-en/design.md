# Paper Rhythm — v7

A quiet paper surface gives the composition its continuity. Color gathers around the device as a soft, functional tint: pink for Home and Kana, sky for Path, sun for Stories, mint for Speak, and grape for JLPT. The typography stays in ink with one brand-pink line. Careful color calibration and restrained detail make the work feel deliberately crafted.

The centered grid creates a steady rhythm across the series. Labels, two-line headlines, and concise subtitles share an axis with the upright phone. SF Pro Rounded connects the headline to the native interface. Each line has room to breathe; the gap above the device is occupied with intent rather than filled with ornament. Meticulous spacing is the main expression of the design.

The app interface provides the visual evidence. A callout enlarges a real region of the exact image beneath it, aligned to its original center, with no tilt. Its scale keeps the original card covered while leaving neighboring headings legible, including Gojūon. The lower canvas edge falls before the tab bar or between complete content blocks. The quality comes from precise crop selection and painstaking alignment.

Kana-chan provides the emotional accent. The opening peek pose keeps its established position. Feature mascots sit lower, behind the device layer, so the rim naturally hides their lower edges. Stories and Speak retain their 270 px poses with an 18 px downward adjustment; Kana drops 36 px. Path grows to 340 px, moves 110 px left of center, and sinks 48 px behind the phone. JLPT grows from 220 to 340 px, stays centered, and sinks 28 px behind a lower phone. Its bottom crop falls in the blank space after the selected answer, before the feedback panel. The illustrations remain subordinate to the feature and headline. The device retains the original SVG hardware details. Its Dynamic Island appears once: the simulator’s baked-in island is cleared from the app capture, and the frame draws its own.

English is a property of the entire piece, from headline to native app descriptions, rather than an overlay on a Spanish screen. Japanese text remains where it teaches the language. The paper, vivid-pink, and ink variants share this same geometry. Each export is reviewed as a full-resolution image and as a small carousel; crisp typography, consistent shadows, and clean separations carry the craft at both scales.
